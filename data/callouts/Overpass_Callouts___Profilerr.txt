The Overpass is among the most popular CS2 Bomb Defusal maps due to its awesome design and wide variety of possible tactical moves. The setting is a Berlin park with a canal overpass. The CTs take the role of an elite GSG-9 team, while the Ts are members of the Phoenix Connexion group, trying to destroy a military shipment. 
The CTs spawn near the cargo track (A Bombsite), and terrorists enter the map at the bottom of the canal. The map provides both teams with ample access to sniper nests, defensible corridors, and cover. 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/o7lg4t-3b68946e8f5b2402c886453509eb303b_Ug8b.png | alt=Overpass callouts]
If you want to achieve good teamwork on the map, you should definitely have all callouts explained and well-memorized. Knowing the Overpass map names will help you navigate faster and simplify communication with your teammates. Including this will be useful for knowledge in CS2 Overpass wallbang spots to successfully disarm enemies before even facing them. 
Overpass Callouts in CS2 
There are a total of 47 CS overpass callouts, often divided into 4 groups based on their location: 
A Site; 
B Site; 
Mid; 
Other Callouts. 
A Site Overpass Callouts 
As the name suggests, A Site includes the locations near A Bombsite. This is a starting point for the CT team, and you should keep in mind the following 22 Overpass map callouts while playing in this area: 
A Long 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/ugykxd-91541d176a77375165cf9befadc412f9_Ug8b.png | alt=A Long spot on the Overpass]
A Long spot on the Overpass 
This path connects A Bombsite to the Fountain. It’s called “long” because it’s the longest way for terrorists to reach A Site. This is also a perfect location for CT snipers. 
A Short 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/5kffcb-44063e3d4de64c3eb3d0be5092b58b85_Ug8b.png | alt=A Short spot on the Overpass]
A Short spot on the Overpass 
The A Short connects Mid and A Bombsite. In case you need to reach A Site from Mid as soon as possible, you should go this way. 
Party/Balloons/Picnic 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/7aeldw-67bbb4eef92aeddf3962c474a175ec42_Ug8b.png | alt=Party spot on the Overpass]
Party spot on the Overpass 
This open area is located near the Fountain and Toilets. It’s a great spot to prevent enemies from entering Mid from the Connector. 
Fountain 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/th3hlx-1fa04e719d761709c00d62428824ec16_Ug8b.png | alt=Fountain spot on the Overpass]
Fountain spot on the Overpass 
Terrorists can enter an area with a tiny fountain directly from the Upper Tunnel. They have several ways to reach A Bombsite from this spot. 
Toilets/Restrooms 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/xsqnil-fa4827c465a7268d913d652aeb592864_Ug8b.png | alt=Toilets spot on the Overpass]
Toilets spot on the Overpass 
These corridors connect the A Long to A Site and Mid. The location may be considered an alternative to the A Short, allowing terrorists to reach A Bombsite faster. 
Long Toilets/Divider/Ticket/Long Bathroom/Long Restroom 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/ge5hxg-c27874638c267a12fb522bdc02cba367_Ug8b.png | alt=Long Toilets spot on the Overpass]
Long Toilets spot on the Overpass 
This side of the Toilets allows the players to unexpectedly enter the A Long. For instance, it may be used to attack A Bombsite from two directions at once. 
Cafe 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/mztmqq-651b1548d2fb1dd005b034709682dda4_Ug8b.png | alt=Cafe spot on the Overpass]
Cafe spot on the Overpass 
This spot is located on the A Long near the entrance to the Long Toilets. It got its name from a sign on the wall. 
Bench/Lamp 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/lu7gz4-20aa565387358756054f9ac268a21f99_Ug8b.png | alt=Bench spot on the Overpass]
Bench spot on the Overpass 
You can discover this spot if you look directly opposite the Cafe. It’s often used by CT snipers. 
Bank 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/j34mgn-050e3e62e6e2a8eb015230d3bf9c46a0_Ug8b.png | alt=Bank spot on the Overpass]
Bank spot on the Overpass 
This callout refers to a building at the back of A Bombsite. This location provides fire control over the whole area. 
Dumpster 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/drceoy-6d0b7e5cec35c336fd944eaa1d318f9c_Ug8b.png | alt=Dumpster spot on the Overpass]
Dumpster spot on the Overpass 
A dumpster located right near the Bins may be used as a cover. It’s also a place where you can hide to take your enemies by surprise. 
Truck 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/qqz47e-c7c31be7bed25577017a0eb3aa76ade2_Ug8b.png | alt=Truck spot on the Overpass]
Truck spot on the Overpass 
The vehicle parked next to the Bank. The defenders of this area usually use it as a cover and observation post. 
Optimus 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/ejvupx-d27ed1aba186e8205564f1f251e6f187_Ug8b.png | alt=Optimus spot on the Overpass]
Optimus spot on the Overpass 
The truck on the right side of A Site is named after the main character of the Transformers franchise. This piece of terrain is a great cover if you find yourself in a gunfight there. 
Bins 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/yvuxcq-6d9b8e1714db76d5fa03c516c00366a6_Ug8b.png | alt=Bins spot on the Overpass]
Bins spot on the Overpass 
At the back of A Bombsite, players can enter a tiny area with green bins. This area leads to the Sink or Heaven. 
Signpost 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/x2qu6y-2bb548433569525128d641162a9ec6b0_Ug8b.png | alt=Signpost spot on the Overpass]
Signpost spot on the Overpass 
Thanks to a large signpost near the exits to the A Long and A Short, this spot is impossible to miss. Sometimes, players hide here to eliminate inattentive enemies that try to enter A Bombsite. 
Sink 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/ls5b52-e03a34287a29fe08ad44e78a06257bfb_Ug8b.png | alt=Sink spot on the Overpass]
Sink spot on the Overpass 
This is a tiny room with a sink located next to the Storage and the most convenient way to access that location. 
Storage/Vault 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/hogoej-1b90eb52b672b41661a10002daa11e5d_Ug8b.png | alt=Storage spot on the Overpass]
Storage spot on the Overpass 
This is a great place to hide while listening for the enemy movement at A Bombsite. 
Long Boost 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/4bbbnk-51014f66d596d4aac9a524fb0764d085_Ug8b.png | alt=Long Boost spot on the Overpass]
Long Boost spot on the Overpass 
Right before entering the Cafe, you’ll notice a large outdoor air vent. By jumping from the nearby bench, you can climb on the vent to peek into A Bombsite. 
Flowers 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/moumdh-044dfa56a819a29c34a04ee97b3ee2f6_Ug8b.png | alt=Flowers spot on the Overpass]
Flowers spot on the Overpass 
Flowers is an area located directly before the main entrance to A Bombsite. The location is favored by campers, so keep an eye on it while entering A Site from the Bins or Bank. 
Close Left 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/3ursdp-fee50d7663116ffc100a9eae839baeaf_Ug8b.png | alt=Close Left spot on the Overpass]
Close Left spot on the Overpass 
This tiny but useful hotspot is located in the left corner next to the exit of the A Long. It’s often used to eliminate entering enemies. 
Default 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/o5w6sb-ae919f9629c47a1e4b99b3f2d3903371_Ug8b.png | alt=Default spot on the Overpass]
Default spot on the Overpass 
This callout is the default spot for planting a bomb on A Bombsite. It’s located directly between two large green boxes in the middle of the area. 
Tree 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/akvrc3-869131284446417326d376038aa45ccf_Ug8b.png | alt=Tree spot on the Overpass]
Tree spot on the Overpass 
You can notice a tree growing on the A Long. Counter-terrorists often use it to hide from the enemies that check this area from the Playground. The bad news is that most weapons can easily penetrate the tree. 
Rock 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/pvof22-4b1093defcee164c69776d6750045b70_Ug8b.png | alt=Rock spot on the Overpass]
Rock spot on the Overpass 
The Rock is located on the A Long, not far from the Tree. It’s a perfect place for terrorists to hide while pushing this way. 
B Site Overpass Callouts in CS 2 
Now, let's discuss B Bombsite. This zone contains several important spots and main areas you should consider. 
Alley 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/vep2bs-52070cbf23052a2b505c359d2dec135e_Ug8b.png | alt=Alley spot on the Overpass]
Alley spot on the Overpass 
The long way from the T Spawn to the Tracks is called the Alley. Terrorists usually opt for it if they want to plant a bomb on B Site. 
Club 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/qbagck-99d96c974b7d3f48297d5d9486c2c526_Ug8b.png | alt=Club spot on the Overpass]
Club spot on the Overpass 
While walking the Alley, you may notice a set of locked steel doors. Players believe that this is an entrance to a club, and sometimes, you can even hear music coming from there. 
Tracks/Outside Monster 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/swbkdi-b3da80589da9f4d1ca3e4c9669ab94dc_Ug8b.png | alt=Tracks spot on the Overpass]
Tracks spot on the Overpass 
After passing the Alley, you’ll enter the area called the Tracks. Here, you can choose between two routes to B Bombsite: the Monster or Short Tunnel. 
Monster 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/ex7z7k-a3aa9dca0fbdfd07c123a765ce558184_Ug8b.png | alt=Monster spot on the Overpass]
Monster spot on the Overpass 
This long tunnel is named after the graffiti on its entrance, which looks like a monster’s mouth. It leads directly to the Sewers near B Bombsite. 
Sewers 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/g7rea6-47b375d368e971347cecadee7aab2eff_Ug8b.png | alt=Sewers spot on the Overpass]
Sewers spot on the Overpass 
As soon as you leave the Monster, you can be easily shot from the Heaven, making the Sewers a dangerous spot to be in. Luckily, you can hide behind the Pillar and make your way to plant the bomb. 
Pillar 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/grbuvx-7db0657fcf0a01a8c580862f06c1ab3b_Ug8b.png | alt=Pillar spot on the Overpass]
Pillar spot on the Overpass 
In addition to protection, this large cylindrical pillar in the middle of B Site allows a player to observe the surrounding area and look for hiding enemies. 
Barrels/Toxic Barrels 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/yule24-bc65fcf7478ea07bd2b5a2a012493050_Ug8b.png | alt=Toxic Barrels spot on the Overpass]
Toxic Barrels spot on the Overpass 
While at B Bombsite, you can notice a set of barrels with toxin markings. This is an excellent location to eliminate opponents entering the area from the Monster. 
Heaven 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/tjw4ms-2db4078c054c3d4e6422d16f18aa8e50_Ug8b.png | alt=Heaven spot on the Overpass]
Heaven spot on the Overpass 
This platform provides vision over most of the area. Considering its popularity, it’s likely that enemies who enter the B Bombsite will check it first. 
Pit 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/igoad5-d0049c5221b81a1d5a6ed82b490bf711_Ug8b.png | alt=Pit spot on the Overpass]
Pit spot on the Overpass 
The dark area covered in the water behind B Bombsite is called the Pit. Usually, players jump into it from the Heaven to quickly change position. 
Bridge 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/da3gsk-6415c6f491b994469db1e79643e3835e_Ug8b.png | alt=Bridge spot on the Overpass]
Bridge spot on the Overpass 
This spot is located close to B Bombsite, Sandbags, and Walkway. It doesn’t provide reliable cover, so it’s better not to stay there if you’re under fire. 
Sandbags 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/5vcjqy-faf7f39a8d62b311d72f1e64606dd489_Ug8b.png | alt=Sandbags spot on the Overpass]
Sandbags spot on the Overpass 
This is one of the most popular spots on the map. These sandbags are often used for cover while either defending or rushing B Bombsite. 
Walkway 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/hk6wo4-15176bcf5225c05b68508562cae6fe57_Ug8b.png | alt=Walkway spot on the Overpass]
Walkway spot on the Overpass 
If you cross the Bridge, you’ll get directly to the Walkway. This spot offers perfect sight lines on the whole B Site, but it lacks any solid cover. 
Boost 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/oklw7x-a43f17fab5646c547d376ed51191d712_Ug8b.png | alt=Boost spot on the Overpass]
Boost spot on the Overpass 
If you want to check what happens on the Water, you can jump onto these sandbags and then jump again to peek. However, be careful as you may also be spotted. 
Graffiti 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/ufrbfk-8ff9c1adbe6847a652168db033ba1e8e_Ug8b.png | alt=Graffiti spot on the Overpass]
Graffiti spot on the Overpass 
At the back of the Walkway, the players can find a boxed-off corner adorned with graffiti. It provides a perfect cover for defenders of B Site armed with mid-range assault rifles. 
Short Tunnel 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/mle2pz-b3c01a1168fe17bf7852929c913cfeb4_Ug8b.png | alt=Short Tunnel spot on the Overpass]
Short Tunnel spot on the Overpass 
If you don’t want to enter B Bombsite through the Monster, you’ll probably go through this tunnel. It’ll lead you to the Water/Construction, and it’s better to keep your eyes on the Squeaky and Sandbags, as enemies may suddenly jump on you from those locations. 
Water/Construction 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/4p775q-ef9cf91a754b57db1d7e57811861b80e_Ug8b.png | alt=Water spot on the Overpass]
Water spot on the Overpass 
This is an area filled with water located on the way from the Short Tunnel and Squeaky to Sandbags. There is also the Boost that players can use to check out B Bombsite. 
Mid Callouts of Overpass Map 
In this part of our guide, we’ll review the Overpass map callouts located right in its middle area. As it’s a relatively big zone with few features, the players divide it into several general locations. 
Mid 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/xoxwmn-fa84b87ac4d1b4d7d8a883286414dd43_Ug8b.png | alt=Mid spot on the Overpass]
Mid spot on the Overpass 
Mid is an area near the Toilets, Party, and Fountain. From this point, the terrorists can continue their way to A Bombsite or enter the Connector to plant the bomb on B Site. 
Connector 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/lt23n5-ad23e7a43a8ad63c8ca7facfeabff04e_Ug8b.png | alt=Connector spot on the Overpass]
Connector spot on the Overpass 
From Mid, players may get to the Lower Tunnels or vice versa. This is an important part of a map, allowing both teams to make unexpected moves to surprise their opponents. 
Stairs 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/3msytc-5c06b53b7d035d14b19a925afe24a0d2_Ug8b.png | alt=Stairs spot on the Overpass]
Stairs spot on the Overpass 
The Stairs connect the A Short and the entrance to the Restrooms with Mid. Sometimes, counter-terrorists use the slight elevation for cover during unexpected attacks on terrorists who enter Mid from the Connector or Fountain. 
Other Overpass Callouts in Counter-Strike 
There are also some other callouts you should keep in mind while playing on this map. They are: 
Upper Tunnel 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/s5nnkk-3b2f9228de4da279f753b4e4ceacbd51_Ug8b.png | alt=Upper Tunnel spot on the Overpass]
Upper Tunnel spot on the Overpass 
This part of the tunnel connects the T-Spawn to the Mid area and A Long. Also, it provides the T team with the possibility for maneuvers as they can enter the Lower Tunnels through the Ladder. 
Playground 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/xn4oen-147fb13e11212b59243085864676713a_Ug8b.png | alt=Playground spot on the Overpass]
Playground spot on the Overpass 
This Overpass callout is located directly behind the Fountain. Terrorists may enter it from the Upper Tunnel to reach the A Long without getting noticed from Mid. 
Ladder 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/clw3mw-d2a85643c769d2bac5ce8473dfefaef2_Ug8b.png | alt=Ladder spot on the Overpass]
Ladder spot on the Overpass 
Players can get to the Lower Tunnels from the Upper Tunnel by climbing this ladder. However, it’s dangerous to use as there may be enemies waiting for you on the other end. 
Lower Tunnels/Blue 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/2bmk4j-cf6773993d11760b40592e492d9def42_Ug8b.png | alt=Lower Tunnels spot on the Overpass]
Lower Tunnels spot on the Overpass 
From this location, you can get to Mid through the Connector or to the Water through the Squeaky. Sometimes, counter-terrorists rush to this location, trying to surprise the enemies and eliminate them. 
Squeaky 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/gxf5gi-3987f765ee1ac52de7068434fe9b5e3d_Ug8b.png | alt=Squeaky spot on the Overpass]
Squeaky spot on the Overpass 
The door between the Lower Tunnels and the Water is called the Squeaky due to the noises it generates when a player opens it. Take into account that you’ll be extremely vulnerable to attack from the Sandbags if you enter the Water through this spot. 
CT Spawn/CT Stairs/CT Connector 
[IMG Overpass%20Callouts%20_%20Profilerr_files/tc7iiq-a7913a8891ce62d7035ba9901f24baa9_Ug8b.png | alt=CT Spawn spot on the Overpass]
CT Spawn spot on the Overpass 
CT spawn refers to the stairwells and tunnels located at the back of A Site. Counter-terrorists use them to get to the Walkway or Heaven as soon as possible.

FAQ 
What CS2 Overpass callouts are best for hiding? 
Depending on the in-game situation and your position, the best hiding spots are Sandbags, Toxic Barrels, Truck, Optimus, Graffiti, Storage, Dumpster, and Close Left. 
What are the best AWP locations on the CS2 Overpass map? 
The best AWP spot on Overpass is undoubtedly Heaven. However, the A Long, Party, and Toxic Barrels are also worth the attention of a professional sniper. 
What are the most popular shootout locations on the CS2 Overpass map? 
Usually, teams face each other directly on B Site if the terrorists choose to attack this area. Also, there may be a gunfight on Mid if the T team tries to rush A Site.
