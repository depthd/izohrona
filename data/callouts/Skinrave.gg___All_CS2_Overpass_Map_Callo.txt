All CS2 Overpass Map Callouts You Need to Know 
Overpass is a notable map in Berlin, Germany, that requires some strategic thinking. It's one of the few maps in Counter-Strike that's based on a real place. You can see German signs and the Berlin TV Tower in the background. The map's design, with overpasses, small hallways, canals, and parks, makes it feel like you're really in Berlin, which makes the game more interesting. 
Overpass was released in 2013 as part of Counter-Strike: Global Offensive and quickly stood out for its unique layout and real-world setting. To do well on Overpass, you need to work with your team, know the map well, and time your moves right. 
If you're guarding B-Site, taking back A-Site, or moving through Connector, naming the spots correctly can really help your team win. This guide will show you where all the important spots are so you can name them easily and play like a pro. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/overpass-calloutssss.webp | alt=overpass callouts image for Skinrave Blog about CS2 ]
Spawns & Surroundings 
T-Side areas 
The T-Side on Overpass offers multiple attack routes and early control opportunities. 
Players can branch out toward Fountain for A-Site pressure or move through Tunnels to Water for a B-Site push. 
Understanding these callouts is essential for coordinating split attacks, utility line-ups, and rotating efficiently. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/2-8.webp | alt=An Image showing T-Spawn surroundings with Upper Tunnels on Overpass Map in CS2]
T-Spawn – Terrorist starting area. 
Upper Tunnels – Stairs that divide into two directions: Fountain and Lower Tunnels . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/3-10.webp | alt=Tracks, Alley, Short Tunnels and Monster callouts located on Overpass in CS2]
Alley – The path from T-Spawn to Tracks . 
Tracks – A messy area that leads to Monster and Short Tunnel . 
Short Tunnel – A small tunnel that leads to Water from where Ts push B-Site . 
Monster – Tunnel used by Ts to enter B-Site from T-Spawn . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/10-7.webp | alt=Image featuring Fountain with Ladders located in Upper Tunnels on Overpass Map in CS2]
Ladder – A ladder located in Upper Tunnels that goes to Lower Tunnels . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/1-9.webp | alt=A photo showing Connector, Squeaky and Lower Tunnels callouts locations on Overpass Map in CS2]
Lower Tunnels – A tunnel from where Ts push Connector or Squeaky . 
Squeaky – Door between Water and Lower Tunnels . 
Connector – Stair that connects Mid with Lower Tunnels . 
A-Site/ CT-Side areas 
Counter-Terrorists ( CTs ) spawn directly on or very close to A-Site , which provides a significant tactical advantage. Unlike maps where defenders need to rotate into position from spawn, Overpass allows CTs to immediately set up on A without delay. 
This not only gives them the ability to secure early control of key areas like Toilets, A Long , and Mid , but also helps them conserve utility and prepare for fast pushes from the T-Side. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/13-2.webp | alt=CT-Side and A-Site surrounding's callouts like Van, Bank and Bins on Overpass Map in CS2]
Van – Vehicle located at the back of A-Site . 
Bank – The large building next to the A-Site , important for cover. 
Signpost – Decorative sign near A-Site between A Short and A Long . 
Close Left – Corner hiding spot on A-Site near A Long . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/16-1.webp | alt=Another photo of A-site from other angle where the Bank and Bins are visible]
A Default – Default bomb plant on A-Site . 
Optimus – The truck on A-site that was named after Optimus Prime. 
Bins – Trash bins next to Bank . 
Mid 
Mid on Overpass is crucial because it's the main way to get to either bomb site. If you want to control the map, you need to control Mid . 
Whether you're going through Fountain to A Long , or sneaking through Connector , Mid gives you chances to move around, attack from the side, or get into fights early. 
Knowing the callouts for everything in Mid helps your team work together to attack, defend essential spots, and prepare to take a site. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/9-7.webp | alt=Fountain, Party and Playground callouts on Overpass Map in CS2]
Playground – A playground where kids usually play. 
Fountain – An area with a small fountain that connects Tunnels with Mid and A Long . 
Party – Elevated area along Mid near Fountain with benches and decorations. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/15-1.webp | alt=CS2 Overpass map callouts photo of Mid, Toilets, Connector and Fountain]
Mid – Open central hub connecting key pathways. 
Toilets – Indoor area from Mid to A-Site . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/11-7.webp | alt=An image showcasing Hitmarker, Rock and A Long with Tree callout locations on Overpass Map in CS2]
Hitmarker – A connection point between Playground and A Long . 
Rock – Cover located at the start of A Long . 
Tree – Tree on A Long near Long Toilets area. 
A Long – Long pathway leading from Fountain/Playground to A-Site . 
Cafe – Wall sign by Toilets and A Long . 
Bench – Situated opposite Cafe near A-Site . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/12-5.webp | alt=A picture featuring Flowers, A short and Toilets exit on Overpass Map in CS2]
A Short – Connects Mid (via Toilets ) to A-Site . 
Flowers – Stairs with grass on each side. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/14-1.webp | alt=A small corner from where Toilets and Long Toilets are visible on Overpass Map in CS2]
Long Toilets – Toilets that lead to A Long . 
B-Site 
B-Site on Overpass is small and chaotic. To win here, you need fast pushes, good teamwork, and innovative use of your equipment. There are many ways to get in, like through Monster or Short , and enemies can also attack from Heaven above. 
Knowing the name of each spot is key for quick callouts and good plays. Whether defending from Graffiti or planting at B Default , learning these callouts will help your team react faster and take control of the site. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/4-8.webp | alt=B-Site and surrondings like Pillar, Heaven and Toxic Barrels on Overpass Map in CS2]
Sewers – The water area that comes when you enter Monster . 
Pillar – Central column on B-Site offering partial cover. 
Pit – The water area under Heaven. 
Bridge – Structure near B Default linking to Walkway . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/5-9.webp | alt=Another view on B-Site where Walkway and Graffiti callouts are visible ]
Graffiti – The wall at the back of B-Site , directly near Walkway . 
Boost – Spot in Water where players can give boosts to gain an advantage on B-Site . 
Toxic Barrels – Barrels providing a view over Monster/B-Site entry. 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/6-7.webp | alt=A photo showing where Water and Sandbags callouts are located on Overpass Map in CS2]
Sandbags – Cover of sandbags along B-Site . 
Water – Water-filled area outside Squeaky , near B-Site . 
[IMG https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/b8/b8b826c7-272b-46e7-b30d-6ad6d0b36223/content/images/2025/08/8-9.webp | alt=A photo from down in Heaven to Walkway on Overpass Map in CS2]
Heaven – Elevated balcony above B-Site . 
Walkway – Narrow concrete path leading from Heaven to B-Site.
